32. Under the 2017 AHA reclassification, a blood pressure of 132/78 falls into which category?
  • ANormal
  • BElevated
  • ✓Hypertension Stage 1
  • DHypertension Stage 2
Rationale

Hypertension Stage 1 is now defined as systolic 130–139 OR diastolic 80–89. A systolic of 132 places this reading in Stage 1 even though the diastolic (78) alone would be in the elevated range — the systolic value governs here since either criterion met qualifies the stage.

Watch for

Under the OLD classification, 132/78 would have been called "prehypertension," not hypertension at all — the reclassification lowered the threshold from 140/90.
