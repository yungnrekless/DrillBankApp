1. A community health nurse is determining whether a family of four is financially eligible for Medicaid and CHIP. Which federal measure applies?
  • APoverty thresholds issued by the US Bureau of the Census
  • ✓Federal Poverty Guidelines issued by USDHHS
  • CThe Supplemental Poverty Measure
  • DThe Consumer Price Index
Rationale

The Federal Poverty Guidelines are issued every year by USDHHS and determine whether a person or family is financially eligible for assistance programs, including marketplace insurance savings, Medicaid, and CHIP.

Watch for

Thresholds are the Census Bureau's product and exist primarily for statistical purposes. Sort it by what the question is doing: eligibility for a program means guideline; reporting a statistic means threshold.

19. A 46-year-old man has experienced homelessness five separate times over the past three years, totaling about 14 months. He has no documented disabling condition. How is his homelessness best classified?
  • AChronic, because he meets the frequency and duration criteria
  • ✓Episodic, because he moves in and out of homelessness
  • CTransitional, because each episode was brief
  • DChronic, because any homelessness exceeding 12 months is chronic
Rationale

Chronic homelessness requires a disability AND either four or more episodes in three years totaling at least 12 months, or continuous homelessness for one year. Without the disability, the time criterion alone does not make it chronic. Moving in and out of homelessness is episodic.

Watch for

This is the single most reliable distractor in the homelessness section — a scenario that satisfies the time criterion but omits the disability. Both elements are required.

homelessnessdefinitions and categories
20. Which category accounts for the majority of homelessness over time?
  • AChronic homelessness
  • BEpisodic homelessness
  • ✓Transitional homelessness
  • DVeteran homelessness
Rationale

Transitional homelessness — typically caused by a catastrophic event leading to a short shelter stay and eventual transition to permanent housing — accounts for the majority of homelessness over time.

Watch for

Chronic homelessness is the intuitive but wrong answer, because it is the most visible and most discussed category. The inversion is easy and it is worth over-learning in this direction.

AMI and SMI are separated by the functional impairment clause, not by diagnosis or by treatment setting. Formal diagnosis depends on a reduction in the person's ability to function as a result of the disorder.
