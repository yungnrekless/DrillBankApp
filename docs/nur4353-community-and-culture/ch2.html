2. During the Crimean War, Nightingale's population-based approach and use of simple epidemiology cut soldier mortality at Scutari from approximately what rate to what rate?
  • ✓415 per 1000 to 11.5 per 1000
  • B11.5 per 1000 to 415 per 1000
  • C250 per 1000 to 50 per 1000
  • D100 per 1000 to 15 per 1000
Rationale

Nightingale's population-based approach and simple epidemiology cut soldier mortality from 415 per 1000 to 11.5 per 1000 during the Crimean War (1854–1856) at Scutari.

Watch for

The two figures are easy to invert. Mortality dropped sharply — the larger number is the "before."

  • B1850, Kaiserswerth, Germany
  • C1873, Bellevue Hospital, New York
  • D1859, Liverpool, England
Rationale

4. What is Florence Nightingale most fundamentally credited with, per the fact bank's summary?
  • ABeing the first public health nurse in the United States
  • ✓Providing the model of trained nursing and nursing education
  • CFounding the Henry Street Settlement
  • DEstablishing the first US visiting nurse service
Rationale

Nightingale is the model of trained nursing and nursing education generally; she is British and predates the founding of US public health nursing specifically.

Watch for

This is the core confusable pair from the fact bank. Nightingale founded trained nursing broadly; Lillian Wald was the first public health nurse in the United States. Do not credit Nightingale with the US "first."

5. Who was the first public health nurse in the United States?
  • AFlorence Nightingale
  • ✓Lillian Wald
  • CJessie Sleet Scales
  • DMary Breckinridge
Rationale

Lillian Wald was the first public health nurse in the United States and coined the term "public health nurse."

Watch for

This is the single most common stem in the chapter. Nightingale is British and founded trained nursing generally; Jessie Sleet was specifically the first African American PHN. Neither is the plain "first US PHN."

9. A question stem describes a nurse who "used a population-based approach and simple epidemiology to reduce mortality in a military hospital." Which figure does this describe, and who is the easiest person to confuse her with?
  • ALillian Wald; easy to confuse with Mary Breckinridge
  • ✓Florence Nightingale; easy to confuse with Lillian Wald
  • CJessie Sleet; easy to confuse with Florence Nightingale
  • DMary S. Gardner; easy to confuse with Jessie Sleet
Rationale

The Crimean War mortality statistic belongs to Nightingale. She is the figure most often confused with Wald, since both are described as working at the population level — but Nightingale is British military-hospital epidemiology, while Wald is the first US public health nurse working in a community settlement.

Watch for

The fact bank explicitly pairs Nightingale and Wald as the two population-level pioneers most likely to be swapped in a stem.

13. Who was the first trained nurse hired to visit the sick poor at home, marking the beginning of organized visiting nursing in the US?
  • ✓Frances (Francis) Root, 1877
  • BLillian Wald, 1893
  • CMary Brewster, 1893
  • DLina Rogers, 1902
Rationale

Frances (Francis) Root was hired in 1877 by the Women's Board of the NY Mission to visit the sick poor at home — the start of organized US visiting nursing, predating Wald's Henry Street Settlement by 16 years.

Watch for

Wald's Henry Street Settlement (1893) is often mistaken for the true beginning of US visiting nursing, but Root's 1877 hiring came first.
