2. A region has fewer than six persons per square mile and schools more than 100 miles apart. Which classification best fits?
  • ARural
  • ✓Frontier
  • CMicropolitan
  • DNon-core
Rationale

Frontier regions have fewer than six persons per square mile — a stricter threshold than rural's fewer-than-99. A stem describing extreme remoteness like widely spaced schools is cuing frontier, not just rural.

Watch for

Frontier is easy to confuse with rural because both describe sparse areas. Rural is fewer than 99 per square mile; frontier is fewer than six.
