1. A public health nurse drives slowly through an assigned neighborhood, noting housing conditions, businesses, open space, and signs of decay or vitality before planning a fuller assessment. What is this method called?
  • ✓Windshield survey
  • BKey informant interview
  • CFocus group
  • DPhotovoice
Rationale

A windshield survey is a method of simple observation that gives a quick overview of a community — described as the "community equivalent of a head-to-toe assessment" — done by driving or walking through.

Watch for

The stem is the classic test cue: a nurse driving or walking through to get a general first impression of housing and conditions. It is observational and preliminary, not a substitute for a fuller assessment if interventions are planned.

2. What is the recommended way to conduct a windshield survey?
  • ABy a single nurse working alone to avoid bias
  • ✓With more than one person, since one can drive/watch the road while another observes and takes notes
  • COnly after secondary data has been fully analyzed
  • DOnly during a community-sponsored event such as a fair or festival
Rationale

A windshield survey is best conducted with more than one person — one drives or watches the road while another observes and takes notes, and the shared experience adds insight. A second tour may be needed to fill in blanks.

Watch for

Option D describes participant observation, not a windshield survey.

  • ✓Windshield survey
  • ✓Key informant interviews
  • ✓Focus groups
  • DCensus data
  • ✓Photovoice
Rationale

Primary data is collected directly by the assessment team — windshield survey, key informant interviews, focus groups, participant observation, and photovoice are all primary methods. Census data is secondary.

Watch for

Census data is a common distractor planted among primary-method lists; it is pre-existing numeric data from a recognized agency, which makes it secondary.

8. A student mistakenly labels the windshield survey as a secondary data source because it produces a written summary the team can file and reuse later. What is the error?
  • AThere is no error; reused documents are always secondary data
  • ✓The windshield survey is primary data because the nurse collects it firsthand through direct observation
  • CThe windshield survey is neither primary nor secondary
  • DThe windshield survey only becomes primary data if photographs are taken
Rationale

Primary data is defined by who collects it and how — directly, by the nurse or assessment team — not by whether the output gets filed for later use. The windshield survey is a primary data method because it involves the nurse observing firsthand.

10. A comprehensive community assessment is best built using which combination of data?
  • APrimary data alone, since it reflects firsthand community knowledge
  • BSecondary data alone, since it is already validated by recognized agencies
  • ✓Both primary and secondary data
  • DNeither; community diagnosis relies only on clinical judgment
Rationale

A comprehensive community assessment uses both primary and secondary data — firsthand methods like windshield surveys and key informant interviews, and existing numeric sources like census and vital statistics.

16. A nurse notes that a certain resident controls who can enter and use a community center, effectively controlling access to that part of the community. This person is best described as a:
  • AKey informant
  • BStakeholder
  • ✓Gatekeeper
  • DCommunity diagnostician
Rationale

Gatekeepers are people who control access to the community or its members — distinct from stakeholders, who are anyone with a personal or occupational interest in the community's life, and key informants, who are chosen for their insight and connections.

Watch for

Key informant and gatekeeper both describe influential community members, but only the gatekeeper controls access; a key informant is valued for knowledge and connection, not control.
